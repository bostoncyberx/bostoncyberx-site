Five logo systems.
Pick the one that feels like BCX.

Each concept is a complete system: primary lockup, standalone monogram for avatars and favicons, light-surface behavior, palette, and type. Every one keeps the lowercase wordmark and the two-typeface rule. Reply with the option number and the full brand kit gets built on it.

Option 01 · The Statement

Wordmark only, one red letter

No symbol at all. The name is the logo, and the single red x is the entire identity system. The most confident and most modern move: it says the work speaks for itself. Scales anywhere text renders.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 wordmark · Inter for everything else

Rule: the red x never repeats elsewhere on a surface. On light surfaces red deepens to #E02D22 for contrast. Monogram tile is the avatar and favicon.

Option 02 · The Signal

Circuit-trace X

The X drawn as two signal paths that route around each other and terminate in nodes, the same grammar as the site's signal line and constellation. The mark literally is what BCX does: two disciplines routed into one crossing.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 · Inter

Rule: mark sits left of the wordmark or stands alone. The paper trace can recolor per pillar in campaign use; the red trace never changes. Wordmark x stays paper (the mark owns the red).

Option 03 · The Bastion

Hex shield with carved X

A hexagonal bastion holding the X in negative space. The most security-forward option, badge-like and authoritative. Reads instantly at favicon size and stamps beautifully on reports and challenge coins.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 · Inter

Rule: the shield never contains anything but the X. Red lives on the perimeter (the defended edge), the X stays paper. Strongest option for bcxBastion sub-branding.

Option 04 · The Forge

Anvil-cut X

Two heavy beveled billets crossing, one steel, one glowing hot. Industrial weight that owns the FORGE story: raw material shaped, tempered, hardened. The most distinctive silhouette of the five, and the most masculine.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 · Inter

Rule: the hot billet is always the one rising left to right (the transformation). Never outline the mark; it lives as solid slabs only.

Option 05 · The Index

bostoncyber[x]

The x held in red brackets, the notation of a variable: the thing you solve for. Quietly clever, editorial, and very ownable in text contexts: email signatures, documents, and social handles carry the full logo with zero assets.

Type: Bricolage Grotesque 800 · Inter

Rule: brackets are always red, the x is always paper or ink. In prose the plain-text form bostoncyber[x] is permitted; nothing else about the wordmark may be styled.

Reply with an option number (1 to 5) and the complete brand kit gets built on that system: identity assets, stationery, social kit, print and digital files, palette, typography, iconography, and do's and don'ts.
