    About this assessment

    How is the readiness score calculated?

    You answer 14 questions, two for each of seven pillars: AI use, data, identity, detection, compliance, people, and resilience. Each yes scores 2 points, each partial 1, each no 0. Your overall score is your points out of 28 converted to a 0 to 100 scale. 70 and above reads as Ready, 40 to 69 as Building, and below 40 as At Risk.

    Do I have to give my email to see my results?

    No. Your score, pillar breakdown, and top three priority moves appear immediately, with no form in the way. The scoring runs in your browser and your answers are not sent anywhere. Leaving your email is optional and gets you the full report and a 90-day roadmap template.

    What happens after the free assessment?

    The free version gives you a preliminary score and priorities. If you want the rigorous version, bcxCompass is our fixed-fee, three-week engagement: evidence-based scoring of the same seven pillars, a board-ready report, and a 90-day roadmap with owners and budgets.

    Rather talk it through?

    A BCX consultant can run this assessment with you by phone and answer questions as you go. No charge, no pitch until you ask for one.
