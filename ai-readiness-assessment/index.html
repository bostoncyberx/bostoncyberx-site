About this assessment

How is the maturity index calculated?

You answer 21 scored questions, each from 1 to 5, plus one unscored question about which AI tools are in use. Each dimension score is the mean of its answers. The index is a weighted mean of the six dimensions, reported to one decimal from 1.0 to 5.0. Strategy and Leadership is 20 percent, Value Realization and Investment Discipline is 20 percent, and the other four dimensions are 15 percent each. Bands: 1.0 to 1.7 Initial, 1.8 to 2.5 Developing, 2.6 to 3.3 Defined, 3.4 to 4.2 Managed, 4.3 to 5.0 Optimized.

Do I have to give my email to see my results?

After you click See my score, we ask for your name, work email, and company. We email your results to that address. We do not sell this.

What happens after the free assessment?

You see your index, six dimension scores, and a natural next engagement. Book a discovery call if you want to walk through the results with a BCX consultant.

Rather talk it through?

A BCX consultant can run this assessment with you by phone and answer questions as you go. No charge, no pitch until you ask for one.
