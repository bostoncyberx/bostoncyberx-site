Where healthcare organizations are betting on AI.

Twelve use cases across care delivery, payer operations and the back office, where agentic systems compound the productivity gain.

01

Ambient clinical documentation

AI scribes listen to the encounter and draft the note, orders and coding directly into the EHR, returning clinician time to patient care.

2+ hours per day of charting time saved

02

Agentic revenue cycle

Agents handle coding, claim scrubbing, prior authorization and denial appeals end to end, escalating only exceptions to billing staff.

30 to 50% lower cost to collect

03

Patient access and scheduling

Voice and chat agents run 24/7 intake, triage, referral routing, reminders and rescheduling across languages.

40 to 60% call deflection; 15 to 25% fewer no-shows

04

Diagnostic and imaging AI

Models pre-read imaging, pathology and ECGs, triaging urgent findings and auto-drafting structured reports for radiologist sign-off.

20 to 40% faster read turnaround

05

Population health and care management

Predictive risk stratification plus outreach agents target rising-risk patients, close care gaps and manage post-discharge follow-up.

10 to 20% fewer readmissions

06

R&D and trial acceleration

AI screens candidate molecules, drafts protocols and matches patients to trials from unstructured records.

30 to 50% faster trial enrollment

The next six value pools.

Beyond the clinical front line: operational, payer and back-office use cases where the gains compound.

07

Nurse workforce and staffing

Agents forecast census, build shift schedules, fill open slots and automate handoff summaries, easing nurse administrative load and agency spend.

15 to 25% lower agency labor spend

08

Payer operations

Agentic workflows handle utilization review, claims adjudication, appeals and member service across payer back-office queues.

35 to 50% faster claim cycle time

09

Supply chain and procurement

Demand forecasting and sourcing agents manage PAR levels, substitutions and contract compliance for high-value clinical supplies.

5 to 12% supply cost reduction

10

Virtual care and remote monitoring

AI triages remote monitoring signals, drafts escalation summaries and runs asynchronous virtual visits between clinician touchpoints.

20 to 35% more panel capacity

11

Pharmacy and medication management

Agents automate refill authorization, adherence outreach, formulary checks and interaction screening across the medication lifecycle.

10 to 18% better adherence rates

12

Compliance, privacy and audit

Continuous AI monitoring of PHI access, coding integrity and regulatory reporting flags anomalies before they become findings.

50%+ faster audit preparation
