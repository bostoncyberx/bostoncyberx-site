01

What the feedback was actually seeing

"The font is not representative of a tech/AI company" is a correct read, and the cause is specific rather than a matter of taste. Four things are producing it.

Now · Bricolage Grotesque 700
Say yes to AI.
a g s y R 8 2%

Editorial display face. The a carries a curled spur, the g an open tail, the y a tucked leg, and the s is narrowed against its neighbours. Masthead traits, drawn to give a publication a voice.

Proposed · Archivo 700, width 112
Say yes to AI.
a g s y R 8 2%

Swiss-derived grotesque on a width axis. Terminals cut flat and horizontal, apertures held open, every glyph on the same rhythm. It reads as instrumentation rather than as authorship.

Costs credibilityPersonality is the wrong product Bricolage is drawn to be noticed. An assurance brand sells the opposite: the buyer should notice the claim, not the lettering. Every quirk in the face is a small withdrawal from the credibility account.
Costs category fitIt sits in the wrong neighbourhood Buyers pattern-match a typeface before they read a word of it. Bricolage's neighbours are arts institutions, design studios, and indie media. The bench BCX sells alongside, Google Cloud, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks, all live in precision-grotesque territory.
Costs the tech signalThe monospace was deleted, and it was carrying it The system currently sets --font-mono: var(--font-display), so every statistic, telemetry label, and measurement on the site renders in that same warm display face. In this category, technical credibility is carried largely by disciplined monospace in data positions. Removing it removed the clearest available "we are an engineering firm" cue.
Costs ownabilityThe wordmark is a font, not a wordmark "bostoncyberx" set in Bricolage 800 is type someone else drew, used straight. No width axis, no drawn correction, nothing a competitor could not reproduce in a minute.

What was right, and stays

The ink world, the pillar colours, the red X as sole identity carrier, the signal line, and the ban on hoodie-and-padlock imagery are all working and are kept unchanged. This is a typographic correction and an application build-out, not a rebrand.

03

The recommendation

Ship Direction A, Signal. It fixes the stated problem without adopting another company's voice or another audience's.

It solves the actual complaint. The feedback was about quirk reading as unserious. Archivo removes the quirk and replaces it with rhythm. Nothing else in the visual system has to move.

It is ownable without a licence fee. The width axis set to 112 is a proportion nobody else gets by installing Archivo, and the wordmark ships as outlined vectors, so it stays fixed even where the font is not.

It stands level with the bench. BCX sells beside Google Cloud, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks. Direction B borrows IBM's authority instead of building BCX's. Direction A sits in the same register as the partners without imitating any of them.

It is legible to the person signing. The buyer is a sceptical, non-technical executive at a 25 to 1,000 person firm. Direction C is drawn for engineers. Direction A reads as competence to both.

One reversal, stated plainly

This puts the monospace back, against the earlier "two typefaces, full stop" rule in DESIGN.md. That rule was a correct response to a real problem: IBM Plex Mono clashed with Bricolage, and mixed faces did read amateur. The fix was aimed at the wrong half. Geist Mono is drawn on the same flat-terminal logic as Archivo and sits with it cleanly. It is restricted to data positions only: statistics, telemetry, measurements, section numbers, and specimen labels. It never sets a sentence. If you would not put it in a table, it does not go in Geist Mono.

Clear space

Minimum clear space on all four sides equals the height of the X. Nothing enters the zone: no type, no rule, no image edge, no button. In the header the zone may overlap the page margin but never other content.

Minimum sizes

Below 120 px wide the lockup loses the counters in the wordmark. Use the stacked lockup, and below 64 px use the mark alone. Under 16 px use favicon.svg, which is redrawn rather than scaled.

One correction

The old label slate #64748B measures 4.05:1 on ink, which fails AA for text under 18 px. It is raised to #7A8CA6 at 5.62:1 on ink and 4.91:1 on panels. The change is invisible at a glance and clears the floor everywhere the token is used.

Rules that do not bend

Red is the identity mark and the X wordplay. It is never a UI accent, a chart series, a section wash, or a button. One pillar accent per surface. All three accents appear together in exactly two places: the hero constellation and the signal line hand-off.

Red on light

#FF3B30 on paper measures 3.39:1, which is large-text only. In the logo it is a graphic mark and exempt, but never set red type below 24 px on a light ground. Use ink and let the X carry the colour.

The monospace discipline

Geist Mono sets statistics, telemetry, measurements, prices, dates, code, file names, section numbers, and table keys. It never sets a sentence, a heading, a button, or a paragraph. The test: if it would not sit in a table cell, it is not mono.

The width axis

Display and headings set wdth 112. The masthead alone sets 108, because at 4 rem the wider cut starts to crowd. Body copy is Inter and has no width axis. Never set Archivo at default width in a display position; that is the one setting that makes it look like everyone else.

15

Merchandise

The mark alone, never the full lockup, and never a slogan. Merch is worn by people who already know the name; it should read as a quiet signal, not an advert.

Rules

Mark only. No taglines, no "Transform with AI" on a shirt, no service names. Garments are charcoal, ink, or off-white; never red. The X is the only coloured element on any piece.

Production

Embroidery uses logo-1c-paper.svg or a single red thread, never the two-colour lockup, because the wordmark's counters fill in below 55 mm. Print uses Pantone 179 C as the nearest match to #FF3B30. Confirm a strike-off before any run over 100 units.

What still needs a decision

Two things sit outside this document. The deck sells AI Strategy Workshop, AI Accelerator, and Posture Assessment, while the site sells bcxCompass and the Transform and Defend retainers; those names should converge before the deck masters are built. And the red is specified as FF0000 in the deck and #FF3B30 on the web. This kit standardises on #FF3B30, which holds its hue on screen and clears AA on ink at 5.43:1. Confirm before anything goes to print.
