About BCX

bostoncyberx is an AI-native firm: we run our own company on the technology we recommend. Our mission: to empower businesses to achieve meaningful outcomes through AI transformation, secured by proactive defense and fortified with unyielding enterprise resilience.

Advisory, integration, and operational services for organizations navigating technology change, cyber risk, and operational disruption.

The belief

Most firms sell fear or hype. We sell confident motion.

Security firms tell you the attackers are coming, so slow down. AI consultancies tell you the future is here, so speed up. Both pitches leave you stuck.

The real job is to build your AI capability and your defenses at the same time, so moving fast makes you safer. That is why BCX exists.

A message from the founder

I set out to build the firm I wished had existed.

When I founded bostoncyberx, I wasn't setting out to build another consulting firm. I was setting out to build the firm I wished had existed throughout much of my career.

Over more than three decades, I have had the privilege of helping global enterprises, governments, and growing businesses navigate complex business and technology transformations. Along the way, I learned that technology rarely determines the success or failure of transformation.

People do. Leadership does.
Culture does. Trust does.

Technology is one of the most powerful forces for improving business and society, but technology alone has never transformed an organization. Lasting transformation occurs when leaders have a clear vision, when people are empowered to achieve it, and when the right partner helps bring that vision to life.

That belief became the foundation of bostoncyberx.

This page is not intended to describe what we do. It explains who we are. It defines the principles upon which we built this company and the standards by which we expect ourselves to lead, serve our clients, support one another, and make decisions.

Technology will continue to evolve. Markets will change. Our services will expand. These principles will endure.

Founder & CEO, bostoncyberx

Why we exist

Organizations deserve better partners.

They deserve advisors who listen before they recommend, architects who seek to understand before they design, engineers who appreciate business outcomes as much as technical excellence, cybersecurity professionals who enable innovation rather than slow it down, and leaders who measure success by the results their clients achieve rather than the projects they complete.

We founded bostoncyberx because we believe organizations deserve a partner who remains committed throughout the entire transformation journey, from defining a vision to realizing measurable business outcomes.

We are not interested in transactional engagements. We aspire to build enduring partnerships.

The model

How we keep ourselves easy to fire.

A consultancy behaves best when the client can leave, so we structure everything to be checkable.

Senior practitioners only

The people who scope your work do your work. We do not sell partners and staff juniors. If your account grows, it grows with people who have run programs like yours before.

Fixed scopes, in writing

Every engagement starts with a written scope: deliverables, dates, and what done looks like. If it is not in the document, you do not pay for it.

Published price ranges

Our service pages list typical price ranges before you ever talk to us. You should not need three meetings to learn what something costs.
