Candidate C

Orchestration. An amber swarm meets a red structuring boundary and leaves it in ordered pillar ranks.

The narrative one, and the only one that uses your existing colour grammar as argument: threats and noise are amber, the structuring edge is red, and what comes out the far side is ordered in the pillar colours. It reads left to right like a claim. The risk is that it is the busiest of the three, and the boundary sweep is a loop, so it never fully settles.
