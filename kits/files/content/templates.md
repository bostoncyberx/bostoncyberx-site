# Content Templates

## Blog post (SEO article)
1. Title with target keyword, under 60 characters.
2. Answer block: 2 to 4 sentences that directly answer the query, first thing under the h1.
3. Question-formatted h2 sections; one idea each.
4. One callout box with the key takeaway.
5. Stats only with named sources, linked.
6. FAQ (3 questions) mirrored in FAQPage schema.
7. Close: link to the matching service page and the free assessment.
Voice: plainspoken expert, second person, no em or en dashes, ban list applies.

## The Signal (newsletter, biweekly Tuesday 7:30am ET)
1. **One Signal** — the issue's single insight, 150 words.
2. **Threat Watch** — one current attack pattern and the move against it, 100 words.
3. **Move of the Week** — one action a reader completes this week.
4. **From BCX** — exactly one link.

## LinkedIn founder post
Hook line under 12 words. Short lines. One idea. Max 3 hashtags. CTA on at most 1 in 3 posts. Card image from social kit keyed to pillar.
