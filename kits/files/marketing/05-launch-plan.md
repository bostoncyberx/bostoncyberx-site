# BCX Launch Plan: Narrative, Channels, and 90-Day Editorial Calendar
**Version 1.0 · August 2026**

## 1. Launch narrative

**The story we tell (one paragraph, used everywhere):**
Every company in New England is under pressure to adopt AI, and most are doing it without guardrails. Employees paste client data into free chatbots. Boards ask for an AI strategy nobody has written. Meanwhile attackers adopted AI first: deepfake CFO calls, machine-written phishing, ransomware that hits small companies hardest. The firms that could help have picked a lane. Security shops can't build your AI capability. AI consultants can't defend it. bostoncyberx was founded on the belief that these are the same problem. We help you transform with AI and defend with AI, in one engagement, from one Boston team, so you can achieve what AI actually promises.

**Launch hook (the spike moment):** "The gap nobody owns" argument, anchored on one number: **82% of mid-market companies have AI in production. 26% have it governed.** BCX launches as the firm built for the gap.

**Proof-of-launch asset:** the free AI & Security Readiness Assessment (self-scored, 10 minutes). Every launch communication drives there. It gives value before asking for anything, generates scored leads, and demonstrates the fused model in miniature.

**Launch sequence (2 weeks):**
- Day 1: new site live; LinkedIn company page refreshed with new brand; founder post #1 (the gap narrative).
- Day 2 to 5: daily founder posts (see LinkedIn system); personal outreach to first-degree network with the assessment link.
- Day 5: newsletter #1 "The Signal" to seed list.
- Week 2: flagship article promotion (Shadow AI), first webinar announced, directory submissions (Clutch, UpCity, The Manifest, GoodFirms, Cloudtango), Google Business Profile live.

## 2. Newsletter system: "The Signal"

- **Promise:** "A biweekly briefing on adopting AI and defending against it, for New England operators. Read in 4 minutes."
- **Cadence:** every other Tuesday, 7:30am ET.
- **Format (fixed):** 1) One Signal (the issue's single insight, 150 words); 2) Threat Watch (one current attack pattern, what to do about it, 100 words); 3) Move of the Week (one concrete action a reader can complete this week); 4) From BCX (one link: article, webinar, or assessment).
- **Stack:** any ESP works; recommend one with API + automation (for the nurture sequence) and a Netlify form webhook or Zapier bridge from the site's newsletter form. Segment by lead source (newsletter vs assessment vs contact).
- **Growth loops:** assessment completions auto-invited; webinar registrants auto-invited; every article ends with the signup block.

## 3. LinkedIn system

**Company page:** new banner (ink background, scan-line motif, tri-verb tagline), About section = positioning statement, weekly reshares of founder content plus native posts for articles/webinars.

**Founder-led content (the engine):** 3 posts/week, rotating pillars:
- Mon "Field Notes" (Defend): a real attack pattern or insurance/compliance development, told plainly, with the move to make.
- Wed "Adoption Notes" (Transform): a practical AI adoption lesson: governance, use cases, ROI, shadow AI.
- Fri "Contrarian/POV" (Achieve): position takes: "Your MSP is not your CISO," "Banning ChatGPT is how you get shadow AI," "AI ROI is a governance problem."

**Format rules:** hook line first (under 12 words), short lines, one idea per post, end with a question or a pointer to the assessment (max 1 CTA per 3 posts), no hashtag walls (3 max), post cards in brand style for stats.

**Engagement routine (20 min/day):** comment substantively on posts from ACG Boston, MassTLC, MassMEP, NDIA New England, local CEOs/brokers/attorneys; connect with every meaningful commenter.

## 4. Lead-nurture sequence (post-assessment)

Trigger: assessment form submission (score attached). 6 emails over 21 days, then hand to newsletter.
1. Day 0: full report + 90-day roadmap template (the promised asset), plain-text feel, signed by founder.
2. Day 2: "What your score means" (segment by band: At Risk / Building / Ready; three variants).
3. Day 5: the objection email: "Too small to be a target" dismantled with DBIR 88% stat.
4. Day 9: case story format: the insurance renewal fire drill and how a vCISO turns it into a routine (honest composite, labeled as such).
5. Day 14: the fusion argument: why AI adoption and defense are one problem, with Shadow AI article link.
6. Day 21: direct offer: bcxCompass scoping call, calendar link, price posture stated plainly.
Rules: one idea per email, under 250 words each, plain text, founder signature, unsubscribe honored instantly.

## 5. Webinar program

- **Format:** 30 minutes + 15 Q&A, monthly, recorded, gated replay (email only).
- **Webinar #1 (launch month): "Shadow AI: find it, govern it, keep the upside."** Agenda: the 55%/26% gap; live walkthrough of a shadow AI discovery; the 5-part acceptable use policy; sanctioned-path pattern; Q&A. CTA: free assessment + policy template download.
- **#2: "Your 2026 cyber insurance renewal is a technical audit."** Broker co-host if possible (referral channel building).
- **#3: "CMMC countdown for Massachusetts suppliers."** NDIA/MassMEP cross-promotion.
- Repurposing: each webinar → 1 article, 3 LinkedIn posts, 2 newsletter items, clips if recorded.
