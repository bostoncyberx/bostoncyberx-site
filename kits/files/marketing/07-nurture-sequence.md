## Email 3: The Objection

**Timing:** Day 5.

**Subject:** "We're too small to be a target"
**Preview:** The most expensive sentence in small business.

[First name],

We hear this every week: "Why would anyone attack us? We are too small."

Here is the problem with that sentence. It assumes attackers choose targets the way a competitor would, by size and prestige. They do not.

The data: 88% of breaches at small and mid-sized businesses involve ransomware (Verizon DBIR). Ransomware is not aimed. It is automated. Scanning tools sweep the internet for weak defenses and go where the doors open.

Attackers target two things: weak defenses and available money. A 40-person firm with client funds, wire authority, and no MFA is a better target than a hardened enterprise. Small firms also pay more often, because downtime hurts them more.

This is actually good news. You do not need to outspend anyone. You need to be a bad investment for an automated attacker: harder to enter than the next company on the list.
