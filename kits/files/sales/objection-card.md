# Objection Card (internal, one page)
Keep answers to two sentences. Numbers over adjectives.

| Objection | Counter |
|---|---|
| "We're too small to be a target." | 88 percent of SMB breaches involve ransomware (Verizon DBIR). Attackers target weak defenses and money, not headcount. |
| "Our MSP handles security." | Your MSP keeps systems running. Security leadership decides which risks you accept; different job, and we work with your MSP. |
| "AI is hype." | 82 percent of your peers run AI in production. Governance is what separates ROI from stalled pilots, and governance is precisely what we install. |
| "Consultants sell decks." | Fixed scopes, published price ranges, deliverables named in advance, monthly. Judge us on the first 90 days. |
| "We already did a self-assessment." | Self-assessments score intentions. Underwriters, auditors, and attackers score evidence. We work from evidence. |
| "Too expensive." | A vCISO retainer runs $4,500 to $12,500 a month against a $250K+ hire, and 40 percent of SMBs say one $100K incident could close them (VikingCloud). |
