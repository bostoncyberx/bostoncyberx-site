# Cold Outreach Sequences
Three-touch email sequences per persona. Plain text, under 120 words each, one idea per email, no dashes. Send from a named person, not info@.

**Touch 1 (Day 0) · Subject: your renewal packet**

We prepare companies for exactly that moment: evidence collected, gaps closed, renewal walked in ready.
Worth 20 minutes before your renewal window opens? — [Name]

**Touch 2 (Day 4) · Subject: 82 vs 26**
82 percent of mid-market companies run AI in production. 26 percent govern it. That gap is where data leaks and failed renewals live, and it is closable in a quarter.
If AI adoption is happening at [Company] with no one owning the risk, that is the conversation.

**Touch 3 (Day 10) · Subject: score first, talk second**
No meeting needed. Our free readiness assessment scores you across seven pillars in ten minutes: bostoncyberx.com/ai-readiness-assessment. If the score surprises you, my calendar link is below.
