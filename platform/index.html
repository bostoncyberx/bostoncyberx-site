A best-of-breed stack plus the people who run it.

The detection engines come from Google Cloud and SecOps, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks; what BCX adds is the layer most companies are missing, senior analysts who onboard, tune, watch, and respond.

One view of everything

Endpoint protection

Network security

How it starts

Onboard. Tune. Operate.

Onboard (weeks 1 and 2)

We bring your systems, endpoints, and network controls under management. You see coverage expand day by day.

Tune (weeks 3 to 6)

We suppress the noise, confirm detections against your environment, and rehearse the escalation path with your team. By week six, alerts that reach you mean something.

Operate (ongoing)

24/7 monitoring, analyst-confirmed escalation, playbooks on standby, and a monthly report. Your operations run whether or not anyone at your company is awake.

Questions

Straight answers

Is bcxBastion software we install ourselves?

No. bcxBastion is a managed service. BCX deploys and tunes the underlying tools (Google SecOps, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks), then our analysts operate them for you around the clock. You get the outcomes without hiring a security operations team.

Did BCX build the detection technology?

The detection engines come from Google, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks, the strongest tools in their categories. What BCX builds is the operations layer on top: onboarding, tuning, AI-assisted triage by our analysts, response playbooks, and reporting. We are honest about that split because it is why the service works.

How long until we are covered?

Telemetry onboarding takes weeks 1 and 2. Tuning to cut noise and confirm detections runs weeks 3 through 6. From there the service is in steady state: 24/7 monitoring, triage, response, and a monthly report you can hand to your board or insurer.

Do we have to replace our current tools?

Not always. If you already run CrowdStrike or Palo Alto Networks, we onboard what you have. If your current tooling has gaps, we tell you exactly what to change and why, with pricing in writing before anything moves.
