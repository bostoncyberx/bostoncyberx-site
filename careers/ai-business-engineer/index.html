AI Business Engineer

Turn business problems into working AI systems, and working AI systems into measured outcomes.

Apply with your resume

About the role

The AI Business Engineer is the role this firm is built around: part consultant, part builder. You sit with a client's leadership team in the morning, map a use case to a measurable outcome, and by the afternoon you are prototyping the agentic workflow that delivers it. You speak P&L and Python in the same meeting.

What you will do

  • Lead discovery with client executives: find the use cases that pay, frame them as outcomes with owners and dates
  • Design and build agentic workflows, copilots, and automation on modern AI platforms
  • Run rapid pilots through our FORGE framework and carry them to production with governance built in
  • Quantify results against the client's baseline and present them to boards in plain English
  • Feed what you learn back into the firm's playbooks, accelerators, and the AI Assessment

What you bring

  • 5+ years across consulting, solutions engineering, product, or operations roles with real client exposure
  • Hands-on fluency with LLM platforms, agent frameworks, and at least one general-purpose language
  • The ability to run a working session with a CFO and a build session with an engineer in the same day
  • Writing that executives actually read: crisp, quantified, jargon-free
  • Comfort with ambiguity, fixed scopes, and being personally accountable for a number

Nice to have

  • Security or compliance background (SOC 2, HIPAA, CMMC environments)
  • Industry depth in any sector we serve
  • Prior startup or founding experience

How we work

Senior practitioners only, fixed scopes in writing, and outcomes over hours. We run our own company on the technology we recommend, and we want every associate to say: this is where I did the best work of my career.

Tell us about something you built or changed, in your own words. A senior practitioner reads every application.
