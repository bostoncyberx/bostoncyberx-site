One team for adopting AI and defending it.

bostoncyberx offers four core services: AI consulting, AI security assessments, vCISO leadership, and cybersecurity consulting. Most engagements start with a fixed-fee readiness assessment and continue as a monthly retainer with published price ranges.

The model

Transform. Defend. Achieve.

You need to adopt AI and defend it with one accountable team, and that is how we structure every engagement.

Transform

We get AI working inside your business. Use case selection, governance, vendor decisions, rollout, and training. The goal is measurable productivity, not a strategy deck.

Defend

We protect you with the same AI the attackers use. Fractional CISO leadership, risk and compliance work, penetration testing, and 24/7 detection through bcxBastion.

Achieve

We report outcomes in numbers: risk reduction you can show an underwriter, productivity you can show a board, and compliance deadlines met with time to spare.
