What's included

Five workstreams, one accountable team.

Strategy and use case selection

We score candidate use cases by return and risk, then sequence the two or three worth doing first into a roadmap with owners and dates.

Governance that holds up

Acceptable use policy, vendor review, data boundaries, and oversight rules aligned to NIST AI RMF, written so auditors and insurers can read them.

Copilot and LLM rollout

Guardrails first: permission cleanup, data controls, and logging, then a pilot with a defined group and defined success metrics.

Training your people will use

Role-based sessions on what the tools do well, what they get wrong, and what data never leaves the building.

Measurement

A baseline before rollout, monthly reporting after. If a use case is not paying, we say so and redirect the budget.

Security built in

Every recommendation is vetted for data exposure and vendor risk, because the firm defending your environment is the one advising on AI.

Questions

Straight answers on AI consulting

What does AI consulting cost in Boston?

Fractional AI office retainers typically run $5,000 to $30,000 per month depending on scope. Project work is scoped at a fixed fee before work starts. We publish these ranges so you can qualify us against your budget before the first call.

Do you build custom AI models?

Rarely. Most companies get more value faster from configuring commercial tools with the right guardrails and data boundaries. We recommend a custom build only when off-the-shelf cannot meet the requirement and the return justifies the cost.

How is BCX different from a general management consultancy?

We stay through deployment: the team that writes the strategy configures the tools, writes the policies, and trains your people. And we are a security firm, so every recommendation is already vetted for data exposure, vendor risk, and compliance.

How fast will we see results?

Most clients have their first governed use case in production within 90 days: policy in force, a sanctioned tool with a pilot group, and a measurement baseline. Larger rollouts run quarterly with metrics reported monthly.
