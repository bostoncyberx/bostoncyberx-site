Intelligent Operations Accelerator

Productivity claims die without a named process and a baseline. This accelerator automates one core operation with AI, then reports quality, latency, and cost against the number you started with.

Who it is for

Who it is for

A function (finance, operations, customer service, claims) has a repeated process that eats hours. You want a measured lift, not a chatbot demo.

What you leave with

What you leave with

  • Named process, baseline, and target
  • Working automation or agent on that process
  • Adoption inside the team that owns it
  • A result you can show a board: time, quality, or cost
How it runs

How it runs

Time-boxed. Pick the process. Baseline it. Build. Measure. Hand ownership over.
