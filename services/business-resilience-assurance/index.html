Business Resilience Assurance

Resilience is not a binder. It is proof that a critical service still runs when something fails. We test the path, name the dependencies, and give the board a recovery story with owners.

Who it is for

Who it is for

You have a BCP document and an untested assumption. A customer, a regulator, or an insurer wants evidence. A disruption in a critical service would be the meeting nobody wants.

What you leave with

What you leave with

  • Critical-service map and dependencies
  • Tabletop (or a series) with findings in writing
  • Recovery path with owners and time targets
  • Board-readable evidence of what was tested and what is still open
How it runs

How it runs

Map the service. Test it. Close the gaps that the test actually found. Repeat on a cadence if you want it as assurance, not a one-off.

Questions

Questions

Is this a pentest?

No. It is a resilience test of a critical service. If a pentest is the right spend, we say so after discovery.

Do you need production access?

We need people who own the service in the room. Access is scoped in writing.
