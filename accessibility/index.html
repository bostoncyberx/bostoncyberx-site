Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Last reviewed August 2026

We want every visitor to be able to read this site, use the forms, and take the free assessment, regardless of ability or assistive technology. Our target is WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Where we fall short, we want to hear about it and we will fix it.

What we do

  • Contrast: text meets at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background, and interactive elements meet at least 3:1 against their surroundings.
  • Keyboard access: every link, button, form field, and the full assessment can be operated by keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator on our dark background and a skip link on every page.
  • Semantic HTML: pages use one h1, a logical heading order, landmarks (header, nav, main, footer), labeled form fields, and fieldsets with legends for the assessment's radio groups.
  • Reduced motion: the site respects your reduced motion preference. Animations and smooth scrolling are disabled when your system asks for them to be.
  • Text alternatives: assessment scores are rendered as text numbers alongside the visual bars, and decorative images carry empty alt attributes so screen readers skip them.
  • Resizable text: the layout works at 200% browser zoom without loss of content or function.

Known limitations

The interactive readiness assessment requires JavaScript. If you browse without it, the page offers a phone alternative through our contact page and a consultant will run the assessment with you at no charge.

Tell us what is broken

If any part of this site is hard to use with your assistive technology, email info@bostoncyberx.com with the page address and what happened. We reply within one business day and treat accessibility reports as bugs, not feedback.
